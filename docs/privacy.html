This policy covers the SmryX browser extension and project website maintained through the-lucky-clover/smryx. “We” means the maintainers of this project.

At a glance

  • Summarizing or asking a question sends the source material and your instructions to OpenAI through your signed-in ChatGPT session.
  • Settings and saved batch results live in your browser. Exported sessions contain the results and source details you save.
  • Extension usage analytics is on by default, with an off switch during setup and in Settings. Extension events exclude source text, page URLs, summaries, questions, and credentials.
  • Website analytics is separate and runs only after you choose Allow analytics. It measures visits to our public pages and install-link clicks. Advertising features stay off.

1. Source material and AI processing

When you request a summary or answer, SmryX extracts the selected passage, available webpage text, transcript, or document content and sends the material needed for that request to OpenAI. Follow-up questions can include conversation history. Long sources can require several requests. The main summary and question flow uses the ChatGPT interface and does not require an API key.

OpenAI processes submitted material under its own privacy policy and your account settings. We cannot promise how OpenAI retains or uses that content. Submit only material you are permitted to share. SmryX’s analytics relay does not receive source text or AI responses.

2. What stays in your browser

Extension storage can contain preferences, prompts, summary caches, batch titles, source URLs, summaries, preview images, and queue state. Temporary session storage is also used for caches and session identifiers. Saved batch sessions and preferences can persist between browser sessions.

SmryX uses your browser’s ChatGPT session to authenticate requests and may store operational session data and a device identifier. These are separate from usage analytics identifiers and are not sent to our analytics relay. Disabling analytics does not sign you out of ChatGPT.

Files you explicitly choose can be read for processing. SmryX does not need general access to your personal files for normal webpage summaries. Exported JSON or CSV files can contain sensitive material; the copies you save remain outside extension storage.

3. Usage analytics

Usage statistics are enabled unless you have saved an opt-out. Allowed events describe actions such as opening the popup, requesting or completing a summary, asking a question, and starting or pausing a batch. The payload can include a feature name, a bounded page count, the extension version, an independent random installation identifier, and a session identifier.

Events go over HTTPS to our Cloudflare-hosted relay and, when configured, to Google Analytics property G-WV65497MC0. The relay filters event names and fields. It does not forward page URLs, source text, summaries, questions, ChatGPT credentials, API keys, or raw error messages. Advertising-data and advertising-personalization consent flags are denied in the forwarded payload.

These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. Hosting providers receive ordinary connection metadata, which can include IP addresses and request headers. The Worker’s application rate limit is keyed to the analytics installation identifier; the application does not add the user’s IP address to analytics events.

Turning off analytics stops future event sends and clears local analytics identifiers. It does not automatically erase events previously received by Google or infrastructure logs. Previously delivered data is subject to the analytics property’s retention settings and the relevant provider policies.

Website visits and install links

With your permission, this website loads Google’s analytics tag for property G-WV65497MC0. It records visits to SmryX’s public pages and actions such as opening installation instructions or following a download/build-guide link. Google’s tag can process cookie identifiers, timing, browser/device information and network metadata under Google’s privacy policy. Our manual page-view events use the public page’s canonical URL, without query strings or fragments, and the referring site’s origin rather than its full path.

The tag is not loaded before you choose Allow analytics. Choose Keep analytics off to browse without it. You can reopen Analytics choices in the footer to withdraw or grant permission later. Your choice is kept in local storage for up to 180 days. Withdrawing disables collection, removes this site’s prefixed analytics cookies where accessible, and reloads the page to unload the tag. It does not delete events already delivered to Google.

Advertising storage, advertising user-data use, personalization, and Google signals are disabled in our configuration. The website does not read extension storage, reuse its installation identifier, or change its usage-statistics preference. The extension’s setting and this website’s choice are independent. GitHub Pages receives ordinary web-request information even when analytics is off.

4. Third-party services and network requests

ChatGPT requests are handled by OpenAI. Analytics infrastructure uses Cloudflare and Google. The project website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Each provider processes information under its own policies, which may involve processing outside your country:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Source pages, captions, preview images, document URLs, and processing resources may be fetched from their original sites or content-delivery networks. Those services receive normal request information. Optional Chrome screenshot capture requests debugger permission. Original page links remain in batch results, and automatic closing of source tabs is off unless you enable it.

5. Your controls, deletion, and security

  • Turn off extension Usage analytics during onboarding or in extension Settings. For website tracking, use Analytics choices in the footer.
  • Clear batch results in the workspace. Remove separately saved exports yourself.
  • Remove the extension or clear its browser storage to remove its locally stored data, subject to your browser’s behavior. Clearing browser storage does not delete copies held by OpenAI or other providers.
  • Use ChatGPT’s account controls for content submitted to OpenAI. Their controls are separate from SmryX’s analytics preference.

You may request information about or deletion of personal data associated with your use, subject to applicable law and our ability to identify the relevant records. Protect your device and exported files: extension storage is not an additional encrypted vault, and no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Eligibility and policy updates

Use requires meeting the age and account eligibility rules of the relevant services. SmryX is not intended to collect children’s personal information. We may update this policy when the product changes; the effective date above identifies this version. Material changes should be reviewed before continuing to use the affected feature.

7. Contact

Contact the maintainers through project support. GitHub issues are public: do not post page content, analytics identifiers, credentials, or other private information. You can request a private contact channel without disclosing the sensitive details in an issue.
