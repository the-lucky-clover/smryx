WELCOME TO A CLEARER BROWSER

Keep the insight.
Skip the scroll.

Turn a long page into something you can use. Sumyst brings summaries, follow-up questions, and a whole reading queue into one place.

  1. Keep Sumyst close.In Chrome, open the Extensions puzzle menu and pin Sumyst. In Safari, enable Sumyst under Safari → Extensions.
  2. Open something worth reading.A webpage, a PDF, or a YouTube video with captions.
  3. Generate Summary.Click the extension icon, the video button, or select a passage and use the floating glass button.

YOU’RE READY

One page.
Or your whole queue.

Start with a single summary, then ask a question when you want to go deeper. Long sources are read in sections and combined into one result.

For the page in front of you

Click Sumyst. Choose your language, read the TL;DR, and use Ask question for follow-ups.

For the tabs you’ll read later

Right-click the extension icon for Batch summaries. Keep source links, export your results, or import a session to continue later.

On iPhone or iPad

After installing the Safari build, use Share → Sumyst on a webpage. The Apple version requires a signed build.
